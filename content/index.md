# Garden Wiki

A knowledge base for home gardeners, built from the Cannabis Cultivation and Science Podcast. Organized in two halves — **Science** (the why) and **Practice** (the how) — with cannabis-specific guidance woven throughout.

Built using the [LLM-wiki pattern](https://gist.github.com/karpathy/442a6bf555914893e9891c11519de94f). The raw source transcripts live in `raw/transcripts/`. This wiki is maintained by an LLM; you read it, the LLM writes it.

## Science — The Why

Understanding the mechanisms behind what makes plants grow, get sick, and respond to their environment.

### Soil Biology
- [[soil-food-web]] — The network of organisms trading energy and nutrients in your soil
- [[rhizophagy-cycle]] — How plants pull bacteria inside their roots, strip them of nutrients, and eject them to scavenge again
- [[photosynthesis-and-root-exudates]] — How plants create carbon currency and invest 40% of it underground to feed microbes

### Plant Physiology
- [[root-hairs-and-nutrient-uptake]] — The one-cell-thick structures where all water and nutrient absorption happens
- [[plant-hormones-and-growth]] — Auxins, apical dominance, phototropism, and why topping works
- [[plant-stress-responses]] — How plants defend themselves against drought, disease, pests, and heavy metals

### Soil Chemistry & Physics
- [[soil-ph-and-nutrient-availability]] — pH as the master switch controlling whether nutrients are accessible or locked up
- [[soil-structure-and-aggregates]] — Aggregates, texture, CEC, organic matter pools, and why soil structure matters
- [[nitrogen-and-phosphorus]] — The forms plants actually absorb, why source doesn't matter, and how C:N ratios cause trouble

### Mineral Nutrition
- [[silicon-in-plants]] — Silicon as a plant nutrient: forms, uptake, stress mitigation, and the heavy metal connection
- [[mycorrhizal-fungi]] — Endo vs. ecto types, plant signaling, inoculation, glomalin, and the 96% partnership
