## [2026-06-09] ingest | Episode 108 — Understanding Living Soil Fertility with Bryant Mason

Ingested transcript of [[bryant-mason|Bryant Mason]]'s second appearance (after [[ep-072-heavy-metals-cover-crops-and-water-ph|ep 72]]) — a guided tour of reading a soilless-media soil test. New/expanded material: **CEC is a calculated sum of Mehlich-3 cations, not a colloid measurement** (and the over-application feedback loop it causes), **organic matter via loss-on-ignition** measures peat/coir carbon and tracks bulk density (ignore it in containers), **exchangeable hydrogen = pH restated**, **phosphorus defies the soil test** (manage off tissue; Mulder's-chart antagonisms barely show in cannabis), **sufficiency-then-balance** with saturated-paste percentages (~Ca 50/Mg 20/K 20) over BCSR, **cation excess ranking** (sodium worst, magnesium trickiest) and **leaching as a hard refresh**, nitrogen **drawdown** into flower, the **evolved amino-acid view** (microbes convert most to nitrate first — value is particle size, not metabolic shortcut, walking back the ep-72 framing), and **letting soil cook** / never over-amend. Created: [[ep-108-understanding-living-soil-fertility]]. Updated: [[bryant-mason]] (2nd episode, Soil Doctor tool + course), [[soilless-media-vs-topsoil]], [[nitrogen-and-phosphorus]], [[soil-testing-and-diagnosis]], [[composting-and-organic-inputs]], [[index]]. **Note for human:** ep-108 and ep-72 are both Bryant Mason and overlap heavily; ingested concurrently with the ep-72 fork, so the two share pages (soilless-media-vs-topsoil, nitrogen-and-phosphorus, soil-testing, composting). On [[nitrogen-and-phosphorus]] Bryant's amino-acid position **evolved between the two episodes** — captured as a side-by-side "Evolving view" note rather than overwriting ep-72's version; worth a lint pass to confirm the framing reads cleanly.
