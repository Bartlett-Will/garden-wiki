# Adam Arnold

> **Quick take:** Founder of Collective Elevation in Bozeman, Montana — one of the largest living-soil cannabis greenhouse facilities in the United States, built from the ground up on a foundation of sustainability and sun-assisted growing.

Adam Arnold is a commercial cannabis grower with roughly 20 years of experience, the last decade of it at his purpose-built greenhouse facility on a 38-acre property outside Bozeman. He came to cannabis from a background in land stewardship: degrees in conservation biology and natural resource ecology from the University of Idaho, and ten years as a wilderness ranger doing wilderness management for the Forest Service. That ecological grounding shows up directly in how he runs the business — sustainability is a stated core value, and "everything else being equal" decisions break toward the lower-impact option.

## Background and approach

In the early Montana medical days (2011–2012), Adam ran an off-grid, solar-powered, wood-stove-heated living-soil hoop house while his eventual business partner ran a hydroponic operation. The living-soil, sun-grown approach won more Cannabis Cup awards *and* was more profitable to produce — what Adam calls a rare "win-win-win": a better product, better for the planet, and cheaper to make once you have a knowledgeable team. That observation became the founding thesis of Collective Elevation.

He is notable less as a researcher than as a **systems builder and operator**: facility design, environmental control, team building, and the discipline of scaling without sacrificing quality. He hosted [[tad-hussey|Tad Hussey]] for a filmed facility tour and openly gives tours to peers and even competitors, on the philosophy that spreading sustainable sun-grown living-soil practice benefits everyone.

## Key contributions

- **Incremental facility design** — built and expanded the facility in stages so each new greenhouse could learn from the last, rather than committing a large budget to unproven design. See [[scaling-and-facility-design]].
- **Greenhouse environmental control in an extreme climate** — managing a 150°F seasonal swing (≈48°F below zero in winter to ≈97°F summer highs) through staged cooling/heating, wet walls, VAF/HAF airflow, and custom controller logic. See [[greenhouse-environmental-control]].
- **Living soil at commercial scale** — demonstrating that deep living-soil beds buffer environmental stress (VPD, heat, CO₂ supply) far better than salt-based hydroponic systems, and outperform CO₂ burners on CO₂ production.
- **The R&D facility model** — keeping a separate small indoor "beater car" facility to trial every new light, genetic, bed, soil amendment, and microbial product before deploying it at scale, which also doubles as a quarantine space.
- **Team and SOPs** — building a "dream team" of humble, teachable people and running the business on the EOS (Entrepreneurial Operating System) framework with an accountability chart, core values, and season-specific SOPs.

> [!cannabis] Cannabis Application
> Collective Elevation is a commercial cannabis operation, so Adam's lessons are framed for cultivators at scale — but the underlying principles (use the sun, let living soil buffer your environment, trial before you scale, observe relentlessly) transfer directly to home growing. He still uses [[tad-hussey|Tad Hussey's]] KIS Organics soil for his veg mix.
