# Steve Cantwell

> **Quick take:** A former UFC fighter turned commercial cannabis grower whose Las Vegas facility, Green Life Productions, runs living soil at scale — and the originator of the bottomless-pot transplant technique.

## Credentials

- Founder of **Green Life Productions (GLP)**, a Las Vegas-based cannabis cultivation company established in 2014
- Former professional mixed martial artist; competed in the UFC (signed 2008)
- Builds his operation around **organic, sustainable, regenerative** living soil practices at commercial scale
- Runs ~500 plants on a fixed schedule — **4.3 cycles/year, ~13 harvests/year** — sustained for over eight years without missing a beat
- Originator and popularizer of the [[bottomless-pots|bottomless-pot]] transplant technique

## Key Expertise

- [[bottomless-pots|Bottomless pots]] — his signature no-dig transplant system that cuts labor and reduces transplant shock
- Indoor [[cover-crops-and-crop-rotation|cover cropping]] at commercial scale — companion and trap plants placed with purpose
- Living soil disease management, including living with [[hop-latent-viroid|hop latent viroid]]
- Standard operating procedures (SOPs) and regulatory navigation for organic commercial cannabis

## Notable Contributions and Claims

- **Bottomless pots.** Discovered by accident when a forgotten seedling rooted into the bed beneath it. Cutting the bottom off pots and setting them on top of living beds eliminated hole-digging (turning a 1.5-day job into a half-day), reduced soil disturbance, and unexpectedly produced faster, healthier growth with less transplant shock and higher yields. Later commercialized as a bottomless fabric pot with Tyler at Grassroots.
- **Cover crops with a purpose.** A self-described "cover crop guy" who nonetheless warns against overdoing it. Start with a neutral blanket cover like **dichondra**; add companion/trap plants only with a clear reason and a defined place. He abandoned **clover indoors** (too much pest/disease risk for the nitrogen reward) and uses **mint** to repel rats (while warning it is dangerously invasive).
- **Living with hop latent viroid.** Argues that keeping plants healthy and unstressed suppresses viroid expression (dudding), that culling chronically affected genetics (his "pure sunshine"/"shiners") solved his dudding problem, and that 100% tissue culture is overkill. He leaves root balls in the bed to preserve worms and biology — a grid-planting scheme rotates planting sites so old root balls break down in place.
- **SOPs as the difference between a good and great grower.** "I grow employees, not cannabis." Written SOPs let him trace failures and satisfy state regulators who are unfamiliar with living soil.
- **Compost tea regulations.** Surfaced the NOP/USDA water-quality rules that gate legal compost tea use in commercial facilities (see [[compost-tea]]).
