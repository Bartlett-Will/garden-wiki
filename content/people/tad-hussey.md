# Tad Hussey

> **Quick take:** Host of the Cannabis Cultivation and Science Podcast and founder of KIS Organics, with over 20 years in the cannabis and organic growing industry.

## Background

Tad Hussey is the host of the Cannabis Cultivation and Science Podcast, one of the longest-running shows dedicated to bridging rigorous horticultural science with practical cannabis cultivation. He is also the founder of **KIS Organics**, a company focused on organic soil mixes and biological inputs for growers.

Tad's connection to soil science runs deep. His father was one of [[dr-elaine-ingham|Dr. Elaine Ingham]]'s earliest advisors during the formative years of the [[soil-food-web|Soil Food Web]] movement. Growing up around that work gave Tad an unusually early exposure to the microbial side of plant nutrition.

After more than two decades in the industry, Tad went back to school specifically to take [[dawn-marshall|Dawn Marshall]]'s applied horticulture classes at Lake Washington Institute of Technology before she retired. That decision reflects his recurring theme: always be willing to revisit fundamentals.

## Key Expertise

- **Organic soil systems** for cannabis — building and maintaining living soils in indoor and controlled environments
- **Bridging agricultural science to indoor growing** — translating field-scale research (soil testing, cover cropping, nutrient management) into practices that work in pots and grow rooms
- **Biological inputs** — compost teas, mycorrhizal inoculants, targeted biostimulants
- **Lean business principles** — applied Toyota Production System concepts to his own business and personal life after interviewing [[ben-hartman|Ben Hartman]]

## Notable Contributions

- Consistently challenges oversimplified "just copy nature" dogma, advocating instead for data-driven biological growing (see [[ep-040-soil-food-web]])
- Bridges the gap between outdoor agriculture and indoor cannabis — many of his guests are agronomists or horticulturists from non-cannabis fields, and Tad translates their insights for indoor growers
- Advocates for [[soil-testing-and-diagnosis|soil testing]] and [[silicon-supplementation|silicon supplementation]] as standard practice, not optional extras
