# Tim Wilson

> **Quick take:** The compost tea microscopy expert — one of the few people who has systematically tested brewing methods, recipes, and timing under a microscope and published the results freely.

Tim Wilson, known online as "Microbe Man," is the founder of Microbe Organics (www.microbeorganics.com) and co-founder of Logical Gardener (www.logicalgardener.org). He is one of North America's foremost experts on aerated compost teas and microbial amendments, distinguished by his rigorous microscope-based testing methodology.

## Key Contributions to the Wiki

- **[[compost-tea]]** — definitive brewing methodology: timing windows (36h sweet spot, 48h maximum), myth-busting (molasses/E. coli, foam, perpetual brewing, refrigeration), recipe simplification
- **[[vermicomposting]]** — the "neglect" method that produces superior castings by forcing worms to thoroughly redigest their substrate
- **The microbial nutrient loop** — clear explanation of how bacteria/archaea process organic matter, protozoa consume them and expel plant-available ions, and how root exudates drive the whole system
- **Organic acids and cation exchange** — how citric acid and similar compounds released by both roots and microbes actually liberate nutrients from clay and organic matter particles

## Background

Wilson grew cannabis for the BC Compassion Club Society starting around 1995, initially using conventional methods (5-gallon pots, synthetic fertilizers, discard soil each cycle). He progressively evolved toward living soil systems:

1. Started remixing and reamending used soil instead of discarding it
2. Moved to permanent raised bins indoors, mimicking outdoor garden beds
3. Developed a stacked bin system (36" × 14" bins, four high, around HPS lights)
4. Achieved **7 years of continuous no-till growing** in the same soil — just top-dressing between harvests and inoculating with compost tea and EM fermentations
5. Got the same or better yields from 18-inch plants on a 60-day cycle as from larger plants grown over longer periods

## Methodology

What sets Wilson apart is his commitment to microscope verification. He doesn't rely on theory, anecdote, or marketing claims:

- Took pH, TDS, dissolved oxygen, and temperature readings on every brew
- Maintained detailed notebooks of all experiments
- Tested every brewer type from 1-liter to 1,200-gallon systems
- Compared air stones, cylinders, venturis, and air lifts (air lifts won for dissolved oxygen)
- Published results freely on his websites

His approach exposed several widely repeated myths: that foam indicates quality, that molasses is dangerous, that perpetual brewers produce consistent product, and that complex recipes outperform simple ones.

## Equipment

Wilson invented the **Microbulator** — a compost tea brewer using air-lift technology for superior dissolved oxygen. Available in 50-gallon and 5-gallon ("Mini Microbulator") versions. He also produces a Microbe Identification DVD for learning to identify soil organisms under the microscope.
