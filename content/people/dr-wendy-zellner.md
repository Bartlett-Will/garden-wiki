# Dr. Wendy Zellner

> **Quick take:** A plant biologist with 17+ years of silicon research, Dr. Zellner is one of the leading voices explaining why silicon is an essential — not optional — part of plant nutrition.

## Credentials

- **PhD** in Molecular and Cellular Biology, University of Toledo, 2012
- **Postdoctoral researcher**, USDA Agricultural Research Service (ARS)
- **17+ years** studying silicon in plant health and defense
- Active with **APCO** (Association of Plant Crop Officials) on silicon regulation and labeling standards

## Key Expertise

- [[silicon-in-plants|Silicon biology]] — how plants absorb, transport, and deposit silicon at the cellular level
- [[plant-stress-responses|Plant stress responses]] — silicon's role in enabling a plant's own immune and defense systems
- Heavy metal interactions — how silicon deficiency increases arsenic and heavy metal uptake
- Regulatory science — working to establish accurate labeling standards for silicon fertilizer products

## Notable Contributions and Claims

Dr. Zellner's central message is that silicon does not fight diseases or pests directly. Instead, it enables plants to express their full genetic potential for stress resistance. Without adequate silicon, plants are operating with a handicapped immune system.

Key points from her research and teaching:

- **Cannabis is a silicon hyperaccumulator** — tissue contains roughly 2% silicon, which is higher in concentration than calcium, potassium, or nitrogen. This makes silicon deficiency especially consequential for cannabis growers.
- **Silicon moves only through the xylem** (transpiration stream), not the phloem. Once deposited in a leaf, it cannot be redistributed. This means root drenches provide systemic protection while foliar sprays only protect the tissue they contact.
- **Silicon deficiency triggers accidental heavy metal uptake** — silicon-deficient hyperaccumulators activate extra transport proteins that also absorb arsenic (chemically similar to silicon). Providing adequate silicon reduces this risk.
- **All silicon sources are equivalent** at the same concentration — potassium silicate, calcium silicate, and sodium silicate all deliver the same plant response. Premium "monosilicic acid" products are chemically identical to standard silicic acid and do not justify higher prices.
- **pH is not a concern** for silicon availability — solubility is constant between pH 4 and 9.
